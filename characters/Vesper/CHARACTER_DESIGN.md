# Character Specification: Vesper — The Umbral Weaver

**Archetype**: Puppet Master / Dual-Entity Offense (900 HP)

### 📖 Lore & Faction
Banishment into the Void Realm severed Vesper's shadow, birthing Solitude—a semi-autonomous husk of dark matter bound to her soul string.

### 🎨 Appearance & Aesthetic
Gothic veiled sorceress in raven-black silks with silver thread loops and her shadow puppet Solitude.

### ⚔️ Unique Signature Mechanic
Solitude Puppet Commands (Controls independent shadow puppet for pincher sandwich mixups).

### 🏟️ Home Fighting Stage
- **Stage Name**: Eclipse Citadel Catacombs
- **Setting**: Forgotten umbral temple beneath a gothic city where void rifts bleed through.
- **Visuals**: Obsidian stone altars, floating void crystals, swirling dark shadow tendrils.
- **BGM Style**: Cyberpunk Darkwave / Synthwave with deep sub-bass and gothic vocal chops.
