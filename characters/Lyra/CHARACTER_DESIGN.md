# Character Specification: Lyra — The Lightning Conduit

**Archetype**: Dynamic Trapper / Spatial Lockdown (950 HP)

### 📖 Lore & Faction
An atmospheric scholar who harnesses electrical friction generated by the Shattered Convergence using twin galvanic conductors.

### 🎨 Appearance & Aesthetic
Agile fighter in insulated navy leather with brass conduit rings and twin electrified batons.

### ⚔️ Unique Signature Mechanic
Volt Nodes (Deploys static electricity nodes) & Conductive Arc (Connects shock beams between nodes for +8f block advantage).

### 🏟️ Home Fighting Stage
- **Stage Name**: Zephyr Spire Overlook
- **Setting**: Open skyward marble balcony of a floating mountain citadel.
- **Visuals**: Ivory balustrades, fluttering wind banners, soaring airships in sunset clouds.
- **BGM Style**: High-BPM Eurobeat / Pop-Fusion with energetic brass and soaring synth leads.
