# Visual FX (VFX) & Audio FX (SFX) Specification: Melancholia

## 💥 Visual Effects (VFX) Elements

- **Type**: Ice Crystal Needle Lunge

- **HDR Bloom Multiplier**: `5.0x`
- **RGB Color**: `#78DCFF`
- **Point Light Radius**: `3.8m`

### Permafrost Barrier (214P)
- **Type**: Deployable Ice Shield
- **Texture Atlas / Mesh**: Procedural ice wall mesh with refractive frosted glass
- **HDR Bloom Multiplier**: `3.8x`
- **RGB Color**: `#3CB4FF`
- **Point Light Radius**: `5.0m`

### Absolute Zero Execution (Level 3 Super)
- **Type**: Screen-wide Glacier Monolith Burst
- **Texture Atlas / Mesh**: Massive 3D ice monolith meshes with blizzard vignette
- **HDR Bloom Multiplier**: `8.8x`

- **Hit Impact Sound**: Glass-shattering piercing crunch + freeze-frame audio stutter
