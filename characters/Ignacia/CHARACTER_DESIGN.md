# Character Specification: Ignacia — The Scorching Talon

**Archetype**: Pure Rushdown / Rekka Striker (890 HP)

### 📖 Lore & Faction
Warlord champion of the Pyre Clan in the Cinder Wastes, wielding wild bladed claw flame arts.

### 🎨 Appearance & Aesthetic
Fierce female warrior in feather-patterned crimson armor with ash face paint and bladed fire gauntlets.

### ⚔️ Unique Signature Mechanic
Blaze Rekka (Multi-stage advancing slash series branching into overhead, low sweep, or ignition explosion) & Ember Dash.

### 🏟️ Home Fighting Stage
- **Stage Name**: Brimstone Refinery Rig
- **Setting**: Offshore geothermal energy rig built over a volcanic ocean ridge.
- **Visuals**: Catwalks, high-pressure flame stacks, oil derricks, ocean spray on lower beams.
- **BGM Style**: Explosive Funk Metal / Breakbeat hybrid with brass stabs and distorted guitar riffs.
